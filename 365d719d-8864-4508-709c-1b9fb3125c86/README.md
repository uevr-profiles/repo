## Description

Fixes the tiny guns and also has the most centered that I could get the aim.
Accurate aiming, the pistol is a bit off but the shotgun and auto rifle were the priority in my book and I will probably not get it any better than this so enjoy.
- You may have to click "MAIN" under the ObjectHook Menu to activate 6DOF. Then go to Config and enable: "Enabled at startup" if needed.
- You will also need Mr Belowski Fix for proper aim offsets: https://github.com/mrbelowski/UEVR
