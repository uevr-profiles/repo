## Description

There's a couple issues: For some reason after loading the preset into the game and starting the game up, it won't switch to my first person 6DOF settings until you bring up the UEVR menu. Once you bring it up and click through the menu a bit, it will go into First Person 6DOF mode upon exit. So strange.

Also, I found the revolver in children and attached it to the right hand, but even after saving the state, it doesn't seem to stick with the preset. Each time I load it up, I have to go back in and re-attach it.  The same isn't said for the right arm (zapper) and left arm (gauntlet). Those have stayed attached with the save.

Another thing is that forward movement often stalls out and character get stuck. Moving the right thumbstick in a different direction changes "un-sticks" the player, so being stuck is never permanent. But it means you're always kind of having to move slightly side to side while you're walking.
